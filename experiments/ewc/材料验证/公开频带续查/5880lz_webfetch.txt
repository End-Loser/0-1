# RT/duroid® 5880LZ Laminates

 Rogers RT/duroid 5880LZ laminates are filled PTFE composites designed for exacting strip-line and micro-strip circuit applications.  

![](https://p16-cc-image-search-sign-sg.ibyteimg.com/tos-alisg-i-h9hire4aei-sg/image/9474eb2659af2d6c0611fa3e1da18859~tplv-h9hire4aei-image.jpeg?rk3s=add9cc80&x-expires=1800131550&x-signature=sXmlDrbGEtk0vQAur%2BtltDI6oqQ%3D)

RT/duroid 5880LZ laminates contain a unique filler that results in a low density, lightweight material for high performance and weight sensitive applications. 

#### Features

-  Dk of 2.00 +/- .04 

- Low dissipation factor ranging from .0021 to .0027 at 10GHz

- Low Z-axis coefficient of thermal expansion at 40 ppm/°C 

- Low density of 1.4 gm/cm<sup>3</sup>

#### Benefits

- Lightweight / low density

- Uniform electrical properties over a wide frequency range

- Resistant to all solvents and reagents, hot or cold, normally used in etching or plating
